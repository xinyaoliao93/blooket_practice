Independent practice

Use your Blooket account to choose a question set and play on your own.

Start independent practice

  1. Use your Blooket account

    Go to the official website and sign in. Follow Blooket’s account and age requirements; ask a teacher or parent if you’re unsure.

  2. Find something to practice

    Look for a question set on a topic you want to review.

  3. Open the set and choose Solo

    Choose an available game mode, then select Play. Follow the instructions shown for that mode.

Have homework instead?

Open your teacher’s homework link or scan their homework QR code. Follow the assignment’s prompts. You don’t need to find a separate live-game code.

If the link won’t open, ask your teacher to check the assignment.
