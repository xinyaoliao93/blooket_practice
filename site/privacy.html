Your visit, explained

A simple guide.
A clear privacy story.

Here’s what this website does with your visit and the choices you make in the helper.

PrivacyLast checked Oct 23, 2026

We don’t ask for your personal details

There are no account forms, comments, subscriptions, or payments on this website. We don’t ask for your name, email address, Blooket password, or game code. Enter game details only on the official Blooket website.

Your helper choices stay in the page

The problem helper changes the advice you see in your browser. It doesn’t send your choice to us or Blooket and doesn’t save it to browser storage. Refreshing the page starts the helper again.

No added advertising or analytics

We don’t add advertising, analytics, tracking cookies, or embedded videos. The site uses its own files for its design and helper.

Hosting can involve technical information

The published website is intended to be hosted on Vercel. A hosting provider may process information such as IP addresses, request times, and technical access logs to deliver and protect the website. This means visiting a website is not the same as sharing no information at all.

See Vercel’s privacy policy for the provider’s practices. Hosting and security features may have their own behavior.

When you follow an external link

Links to Blooket and its help tools take you to another website. That website’s privacy practices apply there. A normal browser visit may share technical information with that destination. We don’t pass your helper choice in the link.

About this notice

This notice describes the first version of ClassPlay Guide. If the website later adds features that change these practices, this page should be updated before those features are published.
