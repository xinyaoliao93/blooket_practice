Meet your guide
Do I need an account to join a live game?

No. You can join a teacher’s live game with their code, link, or QR code without logging in. Independent solo play uses a Blooket account. See the official account guidance.

Can I play Blooket on this website?

This website helps you find your next step. The games themselves are on Blooket. Our guides link to the official pages when you’re ready.

Is this the official Blooket website?

No. ClassPlay Guide is an independent student guide. For your account or the game itself, use the official Blooket website.
