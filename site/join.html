Before you start

You’ll need a device with internet access and an invitation from your teacher or host. You don’t need a student account to join their live game.

No code yet? Ask your teacher or host for the current code or invitation. We don’t create or share game codes here.

Let’s get you in

  1. Choose your way in

    Use whichever invitation your teacher shared with you.

    A game code

    Open the official join page and enter the code there.

    A join link

    Open the complete link sent by your teacher.

    A QR code

    Scan your teacher’s QR code with your device camera, then open the link.

  2. Follow the name prompt

    Use the name your teacher asks you to use, or follow the game’s random-name option. Enter it on Blooket, not here.

  3. Follow the waiting screen

    The host controls the start. Follow the instructions shown for that game.

Something getting in the way?

A code that isn’t accepted or a page that won’t load? Pick your problem and find a few things to try.
